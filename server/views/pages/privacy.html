Privacy

What we keep, why, and how to make us forget it.

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, phone number, email address and delivery address. When you join the cult we collect your email address. When you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp handles that conversation under its own terms.

Why

To deliver your order, to confirm payment and delivery with you, to answer your questions, and, only if you joined our list, to tell you about drops. We do not sell your information.

Where it lives

Orders and customer records are stored in our store system, Shopify. Your cart, wishlist and recently viewed pieces are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us until you check out.

Cookies

The shop does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and fonts are served from our own server. The only cookie is a sign-in cookie used by the store owner’s admin.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email {{email}} or message us on WhatsApp.
