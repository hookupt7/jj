IAbout

Not a brand. A curse.

EOS is short for Enemy of Swag. It is streetwear from Nigeria for the ones who were never interested in being understood.

IIThe refusal

Swag became a uniform. Everyone wore the same thing, the same way, for the same approval. EOS was born as the enemy of that.

We do not chase the trend report. We do not ask permission. We make clothes for people who build their own language and dress accordingly: the quiet ones with loud minds, the ones who walk in and change the temperature of the room without saying a word.

Our graphics borrow from the old symbols people were taught to fear. Horns, sigils, mirrors, the number nobody likes to say out loud. Not to shock you. To remind you that the things they told you to hide are usually the most honest parts of you.
